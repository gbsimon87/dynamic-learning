# Plan 4: Year 3 History foundation and pilot

Implemented on the owner's instruction to start Year 3 following Years 1–2.
Uses the already-approved [Year 3 spec](../year-3-design-spec.md) and
[shared spec](../shared-design-spec.md). No new topic-title decisions.

## Scope

1. Register the complete approved dataset: three units, 22 topics, 88 slots.
2. Map all topics to the saved KS2 programme without changing statutory text;
   record the chosen Years 3–6 split and gaps.
3. Keep History outside the Year 3 award, preserving finished years.
4. Give every planned topic its certificate achievement; lock the approved IDs.
5. Build the four challenges for **How We Know About Prehistory** using the
   existing History engine and DataChallenge.
6. Add a pinned CC0 flint hand axe, labelled as an Egyptian artefact.
7. Support named KS2 period bands on story cards and questions. BC/AD are labels,
   never arithmetic; band widths do not imply equal durations. Keep KS1 defaults.
8. Test and play all four real pilot routes, both themes and phone widths.

## Pilot

| Challenge | Teaching | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1: Meet it | Prehistory, archaeologists, artefacts and remains, careful digs | Choice and sort |
| 2: When was it? | British period sequence, BC/AD labels, unequal period durations | Choice, order, sort, scrollable timeline |
| 3: History detective | Real flint hand axe; visible worked edges; what the photo cannot tell us | Source choices |
| 4: Think like a historian | Find context, supported claims, comparing clues, changing explanations | Choice and sort |

Each card is at most 60 words; sentences at most 16 words. Each five-round run
draws from at least seven templates; choices have exactly three options. All
templates carry answering phrases and retry hints. Ordering is vertical.

## Delivery boundary

The shared spec §10 says **"Owner review before more topics."** This plan ends
at the reviewable first pilot. The next implementation is **Stone Age
Hunter-Gatherers**, followed by the remaining first-unit topics in dataset order.
No later wrappers or placeholder challenges have been created.
