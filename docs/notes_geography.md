## Find the City
Route: /find-the-city
Skills: Locate famous cities on a world map
Idea: “Find London on the map” — tap the map, then see a line to the real city
and how many km away the tap was. A hint names the country.
🎯 Builds map-reading and a sense of distance.

2. Find the Ocean
Route: /find-the-ocean
Skills: Name and locate oceans (Atlantic, Pacific, etc.)
Idea: Click on the correct ocean area after hearing the name.
🎧 Add audio prompts for accessibility.

3. Country Puzzle
Route: /country-puzzle
Skills: Shape recognition, spatial reasoning
Idea: Drag country outlines into the correct positions on a blank continent map.
🎯 Fun geography puzzle.

4. Map Memory Match
Route: /map-memory
Skills: Country ↔ landmark matching
Idea: Classic memory card flip — Eiffel Tower ↔ France, Pyramids ↔ Egypt.
🎯 Reinforces world landmarks.

5. Find My Neighbor
Route: /find-my-neighbor
Skills: Learn neighboring countries
Idea: Pick which country borders another (“Which countries are next to France?”).
🎯 Teaches spatial relations.

7. Weather Around the World
Route: /weather-world

8. Where Animals Live

10. Natural Wonders Quest

12. Cultural Dress-Up
