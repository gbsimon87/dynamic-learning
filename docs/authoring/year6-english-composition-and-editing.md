# Topic group brief: Year 6 English — Composition and Editing

- Registry identity: `year 6 / english / writing---composition-and-editing`.
- Statutory source: `docs/curriculum/year-5-and-6-english.md`, rows 417–422.
- Scope: formal register, précis, passive voice for presentation, subjunctive forms, proofreading and Year 6 spelling/punctuation dictation. Free writing, handwriting, performing compositions and judging a child's own extended writing remain outside this marked app activity.

| Slot | Learner outcome | Model / interaction | Retry support |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spot the supported choice | Visible context plus large choices | Two misses reveal a narrowing hint. |
| 2 | Compare alternatives | Sort into two labelled bins | Reader, main idea or editing focus is restated. |
| 3 | Put the response together | Sentence tiles | Worked lesson uses the same sentence representation. |
| 4 | Apply the choice | New reader/purpose or editing context | Hint directs attention without giving the answer. |

Dictation retains the existing listen-or-look-cover-write frame, so a child can answer without speech. Its specialised response kinds are validated by its own bank tests; the generic topic checker can only perform a structure-only check for that topic.
