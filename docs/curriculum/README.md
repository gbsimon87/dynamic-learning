# National curriculum reference

Verbatim programmes of study, kept here so challenges can be written against
the actual statutory wording rather than from memory of what a topic "should"
cover.

| File | Covers |
|---|---|
| [year-2-maths.md](year-2-maths.md) | England, Mathematics, Key Stage 1 — Year 2 |

## How to use these

**Before building a topic**, open the file for its year and find the statutory
bullet the topic serves. The mapping table at the end of each file does this for
every topic in our dataset. The bullet tells you the boundary of the topic —
what must be covered, and just as usefully what must not:

- Year 2 counts in steps of **2, 3 and 5 from 0**, but in **10s from any
  number**. A "count in 3s from 7" question is off-spec.
- Year 2 compares numbers **0 to 100**. Three-digit numbers belong to Year 3.
- Year 2 fractions are **1/3, 1/4, 2/4, 3/4** only.

The **notes and guidance** sections are non-statutory but describe how the
content is meant to be taught, and they are where the good question ideas are —
arrays and repeated addition for multiplication, the 5 times table on a clock
face, partitioning 23 as both 20 + 3 and 10 + 13.

## Adding a year or subject

Save the programme of study verbatim as `year-<n>-<subject>.md` — year first, so
the directory sorts by school year — keep the source URL and retrieval date in
its header, and add a mapping table from our
dataset topics to its statutory bullets. Note any statutory requirement our
dataset has no topic for — that gap is the useful part.

Do not paraphrase the statutory text when saving it. Paraphrase drifts, and the
wording is the specification.
