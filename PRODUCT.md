# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: children aged 5–11 (Years 1–6 in England).** They play
  curriculum challenges and Skills Mode games, often on a tablet or a shared
  family device, sometimes with an adult nearby. Design decisions put them
  first (owner decision 2026-11-09).
- **Secondary: parents and carers.** They set up child profiles, follow
  progress, and read the public curriculum guides and parent guides to help at
  home.
- **Secondary: primary school teachers.** They use the `/teachers` tools
  (lesson planning, topic briefings, report comment builder, parent explainers)
  during planning time and report writing. These tools have no accounts and
  store no pupil data.

## Product Purpose

Learn with Bix teaches the England National Curriculum for Years 1–6 through
short, visual, interactive challenges led by Bix, the app's mascot. Success
means a child learns what the statutory programme of study asks for that year,
and an adult can see what was practised and how it is taught.

## Positioning

Every Curriculum Mode topic maps to a specific statutory requirement, saved
verbatim in `docs/curriculum/`. The same mapping runs through the parent
guides, the teacher briefings and the report builder, so children, parents and
teachers all work from one source of what is taught.

## Operating Context

- Two modes: **Skills Mode** (standalone, ungated practice games) and
  **Curriculum Mode** (four challenges per topic, progress tracked per child
  profile; all built challenges are open).
- Subjects: Maths, English, Science, History and Geography in every year, plus
  further foundation subjects in Years 5–6 (see
  `docs/CURRICULUM_INVENTORY.md`).
- Live at learnwithbix.com, hosted on Render; production progress is stored
  through the API in MongoDB.
- Public SEO pages (curriculum guides, parent guides, skills guides) are
  prerendered; teacher tools are `noindex`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Learner progress is irreplaceable.** Stored shape, storage keys, topic IDs
  and unlock rules never change without a migration.
- Topic titles generate permanent IDs used in URLs and saved progress.
- Statutory text is quoted, never paraphrased.
- Authored content is not generated by AI at runtime. Content is marked
  "technically validated" until a teacher has reviewed it.
- **Business model: not decided.** No page may claim the product is free,
  ad-free, paid or licensed until the owner decides.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Learn with Bix**. Bix is the mascot who introduces and explains.
- Voice for children: short sentences, plain words, encouraging, never
  sarcastic. Voice for adults: UK English, plain and practical.
- Light and dark themes, both first-class.

## Evidence on Hand

- Statutory programmes of study and topic mappings: `docs/curriculum/`.
- Curriculum inventory and counts: `docs/CURRICULUM_INVENTORY.md`.
- No testimonials, user numbers, school partnerships or outcome data exist.
  Future pages must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The curriculum decides what we teach; every topic traces to a statutory
   requirement.
2. Show, then ask: illustrated introductions, worked examples and
   manipulatives before questions.
3. A child's progress is never lost or reset by a product change.
4. Adults get honest, practical help, and nothing claims review or approval it
   has not had.
5. Collect no data that is not needed; teacher tools store no pupil data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Large touch targets (at least 44px, 48px for primary controls) and short
  instructions for young children.
- Every feature works in both themes, with sound off and with a keyboard.
- A children's privacy and safeguarding review is in progress
  (`docs/safeguarding/REVIEW_BRIEF.md`).
